## Actual runs

Record each run that was actually executed: date, configuration, data version, and command or procedure. Omit planned runs.

## Estimates and uncertainty

Report estimates only after a run. Include uncertainty, seed variability, invalid outputs, missing cases, and any pre-stated comparison with a meaningful difference.

## Failures

Record failed jobs, broken tasks, scorer errors, and cases the model could not complete. These are part of the result.

## Alternative explanations

For any observed difference, state the competing explanations that remain available. Generated rationales are observations to examine. They do not by themselves establish an internal cause.
